About Aaron Patel

With over 8 years of experience behind the lens, I've had the privilege of capturing stories across the globe. From the bustling streets of Mumbai to the serene peaks of the Himalayas, my work is driven by a profound love for natural light and authentic human connection.

My approach is unobtrusive and deeply personal. I don't just want to take your picture; I want to freeze a moment in time that you can feel every time you look at it. When I'm not shooting, you can find me exploring new coffee shops, reading visual design books, or planning my next adventure.
